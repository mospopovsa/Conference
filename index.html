Goal
Make AI in schools less hype, more learning science & practice.

Why we’re doing this

AI is already in students’ pockets and classrooms — whether schools are ready or not. The real question is not “AI or not ”, but what role AI should play so it strengthens education rather than undermines it.

Evidence over hype

We focus on what has been tried in real schools: what worked, what didn’t, and what needs better evaluation.

Teacher‑centred change

AI changes workflows, attention, assessment, and classroom culture. We want honest conversations about teacher roles, fears, and new skills.

Governance & trust

Data protection, student safety, fairness, and regulation (EU + national) are part of the design — not an afterthought.

Want to contribute as a speaker/panelist?
We welcome researchers, school leaders, teacher‑practitioners, and policy voices.

Format

One concentrated day, designed to generate real conversations and actionable contacts for schools, teachers, parents, and EdTech teams.

Keynote + panels
  • AI-supported learning and its real impact on outcomes
  • Assessment and academic integrity in the generative AI age
  • The changing role of the teacher
  • Responsible implementation at school and system level
Roundtables + demos
  • 4 parallel roundtables (parents / teachers / school leaders / EdTech)
  • EdTech demo stations with timed slots for hands‑on trials
  • Structured networking (intro prompts + contact exchange)

Roundtables (small groups)

Each roundtable is moderated, capped (to keep it real), and produces a 1‑page “what we learned” summary shared with all participants.

Parents
Homework with AI: helpful or harmful?
  • Where AI helps practice vs replaces thinking
  • What to ask kids / how to spot shallow understanding
  • Family rules that reduce conflict and cheating
Teachers
AI‑Supported Teaching: how is the role of teachers changing?
  • New routines: feedback, scaffolding, tutoring
  • Teacher fears: loss of control, workload, trust
  • What teacher training should look like in 2026
School leaders
Freedom to innovate: managing the risks and benefits of AI
  • Guardrails: tools, policies, data and privacy
  • When to pilot, when to scale, when to stop
  • How to communicate with parents and regulators
EdTech startups
From pilot to procurement: what schools actually need to adopt AI
  • What evidence schools will accept (and why)
  • Implementation realities: training, support, integration
  • How to design pilots that lead to adoption in the Baltics
